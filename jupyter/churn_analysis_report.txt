MAJOR CHURN DRIVERS (Ranked by Importance)
====================================================================================================

#1. LOW ENGAGEMENT - Total Viewing Hours
   Feature Importance Score: 0.2481
   Finding: Churned users watch 20% LESS content (66.6 hrs vs 83.2 hrs)
   Insight: Users who are less engaged with the platform are significantly more likely to churn.
   Recommendation: Implement early warning system for users with declining viewing hours. Send personalized content recommendations to re-engage low-activity users.

#2. SHORTER SESSION DURATION
   Feature Importance Score: 0.1728
   Finding: Churned users have 14% shorter sessions (49.4 min vs 57.8 min)
   Insight: Shorter sessions indicate content not compelling enough to keep users watching.
   Recommendation: Improve content discovery and autoplay features. Analyze why sessions end prematurely and optimize content recommendations.

#3. LIMITED CONTENT VARIETY - Unique Titles Watched
   Feature Importance Score: 0.1685
   Finding: Churned users watch 18% fewer unique titles (19.5 vs 23.7)
   Insight: Users exploring less content diversity are not finding enough value.
   Recommendation: Diversify recommendations. Create themed collections to encourage exploration of different content.

#4. CUSTOMER SERVICE ISSUES
   Feature Importance Score: 0.1292
   Finding: Churned users have 28% MORE customer service interactions (3.18 vs 2.49)
   Insight: High CS interactions correlate with dissatisfaction and technical/billing issues.
   Recommendation: Improve first-contact resolution. Proactively reach out to users with 3+ CS contacts to address underlying issues.

#5. CONTENT GENRE PREFERENCES
   Feature Importance Score: 0.1087
   Finding: Horror (52.3% churn) and Thriller (48.3% churn) genres show highest churn rates
   Insight: Certain genre preferences correlate with higher churn, possibly due to content library gaps.
   Recommendation: Expand content library in high-churn genres. Cross-promote related genres to diversify user interests.

====================================================================================================
CONCLUSION
====================================================================================================

The analysis reveals that churn is fundamentally an ENGAGEMENT problem, not a pricing problem.
Users who churn show consistent patterns of lower engagement across all metrics:

  • 20% less viewing time
  • 18% fewer unique titles explored
  • 14% shorter session durations
  • 20% fewer binge-watching sessions

The presence of elevated customer service interactions (+28%) among churned users suggests
that technical issues or service problems accelerate churn decisions for already-disengaged users.

SUCCESS METRICS TO TRACK:
  1. Reduce average viewing hour decline rate by 30% within 90 days
  2. Increase unique titles per user by 15% for at-risk segments
  3. Reduce CS interactions per user from 2.8 to <2.0
  4. Lower Basic tier churn from 43.5% to <35% within 6 months
  5. Overall churn reduction target: 38.6% → 30% (22% improvement)
